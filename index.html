About Me

I am a Mechanical Engineering graduate from Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), Bangladesh, with research interests in thermal and electrochemical energy storage, heat transfer, and sustainable energy systems.

My research experience includes phase-change-material-based thermal energy storage, slurry flow electrodes for electrochemical energy storage, and thermal comfort assessment. I am also gaining industrial engineering experience as an Engineering Officer at Renata PLC.

I am preparing for fully funded PhD opportunities in Mechanical Engineering for Fall 2027, with a focus on energy storage, thermal management, and related sustainable energy technologies.
