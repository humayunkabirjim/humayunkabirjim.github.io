Research Projects

Selected projects reflecting my research interests and experimental experience.

Slurry Flow Electrodes for Energy Storage

Research on slurry-based electrode materials and their potential for scalable electrochemical energy storage. My work involves electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS), Nyquist plot interpretation, equivalent-circuit analysis, and evaluation of charge-transfer resistance.

Materials investigated include aluminium oxide, sodium thiosulfate, graphene oxide, and zinc oxide in different aqueous electrolytes.

EISElectrochemistry Energy Storage

Bio-inspired Thermal Energy Storage

Undergraduate thesis: “Thermal Energy Storage Enhancement using a Bio-inspired Cylindrical Structure with Hybrid Phase Change Materials.” The project investigated heat-storage enhancement using paraffin, sodium acetate trihydrate, aluminium chips, and chain-mesh structures.

Experimental investigations involved solar thermal heating arrangements and comparisons of thermal performance.

Phase Change Materials Solar Thermal Heat Transfer

Thermal Comfort in Greenhouse Workplaces

Research involving environmental thermal comfort indicators, including WBGT, mean radiant temperature, PMV, and PPD, with machine-learning approaches for thermal comfort prediction.

Thermal Comfort Machine Learning

Photovoltaic Performance and Environmental Effects

Review research on how dust, humidity, and design-related factors influence photovoltaic system performance, reliability, and energy production.
